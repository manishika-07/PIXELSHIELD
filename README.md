# 🛡️ Photo Shield: Protect Before You Post

> Early defence against deepfakes and altered photos: protection, detection and response in one simple tool.


## The problem
A single public photo is enough to create fake nude images, face-swap videos or a
face-copying model. Victims usually find out after the harm has spread, and reporting
is slow and fragmented across apps. Students, young women, creators and families
are the most exposed.

## Our solution
Photo Shield acts **before** the harm. It adds an invisible protective layer to your
photo that looks unchanged to people but disrupts AI misuse, then helps you watch for
copies and respond quickly if something goes wrong.

| Layer | What it does | Features |
|---|---|---|
| 🛡️ **Protect** | Invisible shield against face recognition and AI editing, stress-tested against WhatsApp/Instagram compression | 1–5 |
| 👀 **Watch** | Opt-in misuse alerts and privacy-safe photo fingerprints (StopNCII-style) | 6–7 |
| 🚨 **Respond** | Sealed evidence vault, guided takedown complaints, "Is this fake?" checker, blackmail emergency mode | 8–11 |
| 🤝 **Reach** | Adjustable shield strength; Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu and English | 12–13 |

## How it works
- **Face-recognition shield:** Fawkes-style PGD cloaking that pushes face embeddings away from the real identity.
- **Editing shield:** PhotoGuard-style attack on the Stable Diffusion encoder, so AI edits come out garbled.
- **Survives the real world:** optimized over 60 steps with resize, blur, noise and JPEG transforms.
- **Real measurements:** the stress-test score and forensic heatmaps are computed, not simulated.

## Privacy by design
- **On-device mode (default):** everything runs in the browser and photos never leave your phone.
- **Enhanced mode (opt-in):** photos are processed in memory over HTTPS and deleted immediately.
- No accounts. Only hashes and sealed evidence metadata are ever stored.

## Honest limits
Photo Shield makes AI misuse much harder, not impossible, like a lock on a door.
It is not a guarantee, and "Is this fake?" results are strong hints, not legal proof.

## Tech stack
FastAPI · PyTorch · facenet-pytorch · diffusers (SD VAE) · OpenCV · SQLite · Docker
Frontend: vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, with a light/dark theme and 6 Indian languages.
